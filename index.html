About Us

Management Information Systems Association (MISA) at the David Nazarian College of Business and Economics at CSUN is a non-profit student organization open to all students interested in the information systems industry.

We aim to give students meaningful opportunities to learn, grow, and connect through industry speakers, technical meetings, professional workshops, networking opportunities, and more.
